About WELMORA

Empowering women through better wellness, thoughtful products, sustainable choices and accessible healthcare solutions.

Wellness, Made For Women

Welmora was founded on a simple idea: women's health deserves products that are thoughtfully made, honestly labelled, and genuinely effective — not just marketed that way.

From sustainable menstrual care to everyday nutrition and hormonal wellbeing, every product in our range is chosen to support women through every stage of life.

  • Sustainably made, reusable menstrual care
  • Nutrition designed for women's needs
  • Transparent ingredients, no hidden claims
  • Trusted by workplaces and communities through our CSR programs

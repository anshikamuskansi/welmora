Shipping, policies & frequently asked questions

Everything wholesale buyers usually ask us before placing a first order.

Shipping & returns

Orders typically ship within 3–7 business days depending on quantity and destination. Exact delivery timelines are confirmed at the time of quote.

Because this is a wholesale supplier, returns are handled case-by-case — please contact us within 48 hours of delivery if a shipment arrives damaged or incorrect, and we'll arrange a replacement or credit.

Privacy policy

We only collect the business and contact details you share with us (name, company, email, phone) to respond to quote requests and process orders. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

If you'd like your details removed from our records, email orders@fieldandform.example and we'll action it within a few business days.

Frequently asked questions

Do you sell to individual customers, or only businesses?

We currently supply wholesale only — pharmacies, gyms, clinics, and retail chains. If you're an individual looking for a single item, we can point you to one of our retail partners.

What's the minimum order quantity?

MOQs vary by product and are shown on each product's listing — most start around 50 units. Contact us if you need a smaller trial order.

Are your products certified?

Yes — our protein products are FSSAI-licensed, and our menstrual cups are made of certified medical-grade silicone. Certification documents are available on request.

How do I get a price quote?

Fill out the form on our Contact page with the products and quantities you need — we typically reply within one business day.

Can I get GST invoicing?

Yes, GST invoices are available for all wholesale orders — just share your GSTIN when placing an order.
